### Why I started Spelling Creator

My brother has always had autism and can't speak either.
But I always knew he was in there.

But recently we started spelling and it's been a miracle.
We can't ask open ended questions yet (per the practitioner) but he can spell words
from lessons even my mother can't spell.

One day on a walk with my family I had an idea.
What if you could make lessons easily and find ones others had made?

So it began.

### Why I Hope This Helps People

It was not just for my brother from the beginning.
I wanted people from all over to recieve the benefits!

So I hope this helps families in need.

ABA facilties have been essentially trapping nonspeaker's souls in limbo.

It's torture, espeically for smart beings like my brother.

I hope we can rid the world of greed and tyranny forever and together.
